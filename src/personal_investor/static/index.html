Bond & GIC examples & where to buy

In Canada, individual bonds and GICs are bought through a bank or an online brokerage. Bond ETFs trade on the TSX like stocks. Consider holding them in a registered account: TFSA, RRSP or FHSA.

Educational tool only — not financial advice. Projections use simplified long-run assumptions and a Monte Carlo simulation (1,000 runs, monthly steps; yearly contributions are added at each year end); crypto is far more volatile than the gold/commodities assumption. All values are in Canadian dollars. TSX prices come from TMX Money; US prices come from Nasdaq (may be delayed about 15 minutes) and are converted to CAD at a live USD/CAD rate (Coinbase, refreshed every minute; the Bank of Canada's daily rate is used if that's unavailable), so their CAD value also moves with the exchange rate. Crypto prices come from CoinGecko in CAD; Government of Canada yields come from the Bank of Canada. Taxes, fees and currency-conversion costs are not included. You can lose money.
